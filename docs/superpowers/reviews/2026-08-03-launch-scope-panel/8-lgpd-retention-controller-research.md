# LGPD research: retention & controller structure for T.A.R.S. / Ella

*Commissioned 2026-08-03 after owner review of the launch-scope spec ("use regulation, research if needed, use best practices"). Web-researched with cited sources; supports spec §6 and §8. Compliance analysis, not legal advice.*

## 1. Retention of recruitment data

**Statutory baseline.** LGPD ties retention to purpose: processing must be limited to the minimum necessary ([Art. 6, III](https://www.planalto.gov.br/ccivil_03/_ato2015-2018/2018/lei/l13709.htm)); processing terminates when "a finalidade foi alcançada" (Art. 15, I); and data "serão eliminados após o término de seu tratamento", with retention allowed only for legal obligation (Art. 16, I), transfer to third parties (III), or the controller's exclusive use "desde que anonimizados os dados" (IV). **No ANPD guidance specific to recruitment retention was found** (gov.br/anpd under maintenance on 2026-08-03; searches surfaced none — this gap is real, not smoothed over).

**Brazilian market practice (numbers).** Guidance converges on "delete at search close" as default: "os dados coletados devem ser apagados tão logo acabe o processo seletivo, caso o candidato não seja contratado" ([lgpdbrasil.com.br](https://lgpdbrasil.com.br/lgpd-5-cuidados-no-processo-de-selecao-de-novos-colaboradores/)). [Migalhas](https://www.migalhas.com.br/depeso/363808/o-tratamento-dos-curriculos-na-lgpd) accepts keeping CVs for future roles given explicit purpose + **maximum period (its example: 2 years)** + express consent. The [FGV HR guide (Oct/2023)](https://portal.fgv.br/sites/default/files/uploads/recursos_humanos.pdf) says candidate-only artifacts (tests, motivation letters) "não devem ser armazenados" after selection, and a talent pool requires a privacy notice plus standing deletion rights.

**The labor-prescription anchor — partially confirmed.** FGV grounds retention in "dois importantes prazos prescricionais: o quinquenal... e o bienal (art. 7º, XXIX da Constituição Federal)" — but **for employee/ex-employee data, not candidates**. No reputable Brazilian source recommends 5-year retention of *candidate* data; the Brazilian literature is thin here. The closest authority is France's CNIL model (cited by the industry body WEC): active retention **max 2 years from last contact**, plus **"intermediate archiving for a period of five years from the date on which the position was filled, with strictly limited access"** specifically for discrimination-litigation defense ([Claeys & Engels on CNIL](https://www.claeysengels.be/en-gb/news-events/how-long-should-hr-data-be-retained-when-law-silent-cnil-guidelines-useful-reference)). Defense-of-claims retention maps to LGPD Art. 7, VI (exercise of rights in judicial proceedings).

> **Recommended policy (enforceable):**
> - **(a) Interview transcripts:** delete **90 days** after report delivery (keep current default, but enforce it). Verbatim speech is the most invasive artifact and no source supports long retention; necessity ends when the report is produced (Art. 15, I).
> - **(b) Recruiter notes:** move to restricted archive at search close; delete at **2 years** (CNIL active-phase analogy; bienal prescription window).
> - **(c) Assessment reports delivered to clients:** restricted archive, delete at **5 years** from search close — the only artifact worth keeping as evidence of non-discriminatory selection (Art. 7, VI; CNIL 5-year intermediate archive). Access: legal/DPO only.
> - **(d) CVs:** delete at search close **unless** the candidate opts into the talent pool by specific consent stating a period (recommend **2 years, renewable on new contact** per Migalhas/CNIL practice). Talent pool is a separate processing with its own basis — never a silent default.
> - **At expiry: delete, don't "anonymize."** Art. 16, IV permits keeping only truly anonymized data for exclusive use — aggregate product metrics qualify; a de-named transcript does not.

## 2. Controller structure: Ella vs. client

**LGPD/ANPD.** Controlador is the one "a quem competem as decisões referentes ao tratamento"; operador "realiza o tratamento... em nome do controlador" (Art. 5, VI–VII). The [ANPD Guia Orientativo — Agentes de Tratamento, v2.0, Apr/2022](https://egov.df.gov.br/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Guia-Orientativo-%E2%80%93-Definicoes-dos-agentes-de-Tratamento-de-Dados-Pessoais-e-dos-Encarregados-%E2%80%93-ANPD.pdf) (mirror; gov.br in maintenance) makes the test decision power over **essential elements**: finalidade, nature of data, and *duração/prazo de eliminação* (¶¶34–38); an operador "só pode agir no limite das finalidades determinadas pelo controlador" (¶53). **Confirmed: the LGPD "não explicite o conceito de controladoria conjunta"** — the ANPD infers it from Art. 42, §1º, II (solidary liability) and requires three cumulative criteria, including *common or convergent* decisions on purposes (¶¶41, 49–50). Distinct purposes over the same data ⇒ "controladores singulares" (¶45).

**GDPR market practice.** The World Employment Confederation's ["Controller or Processor?" guidelines](https://wecglobal.org/uploads/2019/07/WEC-guidelines-data-processing-roles-2024.pdf) conclude for direct recruitment/search: *"The parties are Independent Controllers; the recruitment agency is the Controller for their own processing and the client is the Controller for their own processing"* (p. 13) — because the agency sources candidates, decides methods, and maintains its own pool. Processor status applies only to true RPO (recruiting in the client's name/systems, p. 14). UK practice agrees ([recLAW](https://reclaw.co.uk/legal-advice/data-controllers-vs-processors-in-recruitment-agencies/)); the ICO's 2024 AI-recruitment audits flagged controller/processor confusion as a top compliance failure ([Osborne Clarke](https://www.osborneclarke.com/insights/gdpr-hr-uk-ico-audits-ai-recruitment-tools-and-english-high-court-defines-controllers)).

Ella decides how interviews are run, what T.A.R.S. captures, retention, and methodology — all essential elements. That is controller conduct, not operador.

> **Recommended allocation (for LIA + privacy notice):**
> - **Ella = controller** for search execution: interviews, transcripts, notes, reports, talent pool.
> - **Client = separate (singular/independent) controller** for its own processing upon receiving the report. Not joint: purposes are linked but distinct (ANPD ¶45; WEC p. 13). Avoid shared candidate-pool systems with clients — that is the fact pattern that creates controladoria conjunta and solidary liability.
> - **Contract clause:** "As partes atuam como controladoras singulares e independentes: a Ella é controladora dos dados tratados na condução da busca (entrevistas, transcrições, notas e relatórios); o Cliente torna-se controlador independente dos dados contidos no relatório a partir de seu recebimento, respondendo por sua própria base legal, retenção e atendimento a direitos dos titulares, vedado o uso para finalidade diversa da decisão de contratação."

**Caveats:** roles are per-activity — if a client ever mandates Ella to work inside the client's ATS under client rules, that slice is operador work (WEC, ANPD Exemplo 5). Sources conflict mildly on candidate-CV retention (immediate deletion vs. 90–180 days vs. 2 years with consent); the policy above takes the conservative default with consent-based extension.
